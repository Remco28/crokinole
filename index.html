How to play

Choose a seated view, then get ready. Tap the shooting line to place your disc. Start your finger behind the disc, even outside the rim, and flick through it. The disc launches when you make contact; follow through naturally.

When an opponent has a disc on the board, you must hit it, directly or through another disc. Otherwise, a disc involved in your shot must finish touching or inside the inner ring, or sink a 20.

Invalid shots remove your involved discs. Rings score 15, 10, and 5; touching a line scores lower. Two players and teams earn the difference each round. Free-for-all players earn their own points. First to 100 wins; ties continue.
